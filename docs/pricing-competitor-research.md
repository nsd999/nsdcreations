# NSD Creations — Competitor Pricing Research

**Checked:** 22 October 2026  
**Currency:** INR  
**Purpose:** Comparative price evidence for quoting; not a declaration that NSD's current prices are profitable.

## How to use this research

Prices below are publicly displayed prices or starting/indicative prices found on the linked provider pages as of the date above. Package scope varies: a monthly retainer is not comparable with a one-off setup; a fixed 30-second AI ad is not the same as a custom product commercial; per-page collateral cannot be compared directly with a brochure bundle. Taxes, media budgets, usage rights, hosting, third-party API or WhatsApp fees, revisions and source files are only included when the source explicitly states them.

The companion workbook `nsd_creations_pricing_cost_model.xlsx` retains 75 itemized price observations with a URL, service, billing unit, scope notes/exclusions and source confidence. The price list in this document is a selected summary, not an exhaustive internet survey.

## Market interpretation for NSD Creations

1. **Graphic design:** Low-cost, single-output offers exist, but multi-concept branding, source files, print preparation and revision limits substantially change the effective price. The current NSD ₹299 poster / ₹499 graphic prices should be treated as hypotheses to cost-test, not automatically as the standard commercial quote.
2. **AI video:** Public offers range from fixed short-form AI video packs (e.g. OnsetFrame) to higher-priced promo or custom UGC work. NSD should define duration, language, number of scenes, voice, captions, output aspect ratios, licensing/usage and revisions before comparing a ₹4,999 ad or ₹7,999 product commercial.
3. **Marketing:** Low-entry SEO and social prices tend to be scoped retainers. Avoid bundling ad spend into the management fee and specify monthly output volumes, locations, channels and reports.
4. **Automation:** A vendor's subscription price is not equivalent to implementation. Separate build/setup, monthly support, message/conversation charges, CRM licences, hosting and any usage-based API fees. Don't promise Cloud API automation based on WhatsApp Business mobile-app access alone.
5. **Web/software:** Starting prices vary with page count, ecommerce, custom integrations, data migration, authentication, admin roles, QA and maintenance. Every quote should specify acceptance criteria, exclusions and post-launch support.
6. **Music/creator and education:** The available evidence does not provide a reliable equivalent public price for NSD's specific lyric-video and educational-worksheet scopes. Those additions therefore remain **Custom Quote** until the owner validates real labour, revision and rights costs. Digital corporate greeting cards are also custom-quoted by recipient count, number of templates and personalisation rules.

## Recommended pricing approval rule

Keep the user's suggested figures as editable **hypotheses**, not as confirmed profitable prices. For each scope, fill in and owner-validate estimated delivery hours, loaded labour rate, contractor charges, software/API charges, other direct costs, revision/rework allowance, payment fees and target gross margin in the companion workbook.

Use:

`Minimum quote = Total delivery cost / (1 - target gross margin)`

Where percentage-based payment processing is charged on the sale price, calculate the fee algebraically in the cost model so the fee itself is covered; the workbook uses that gross-up. Founder time must have a cost. Third-party media budgets, platform subscriptions, messaging fees and licences should be itemised unless explicitly included.
